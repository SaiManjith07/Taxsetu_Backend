# Prompt to create an invoice image for OCR testing

Use this prompt in an **AI image generator** (e.g. DALL·E, Midjourney, Ideogram, or Gemini image gen) to get a realistic Indian GST invoice image for testing the Photo Upload OCR feature.

---

## Prompt (copy-paste)

```
A clean, realistic Indian GST tax invoice document on white A4 paper, flat lay or slightly angled. The invoice has a letterhead at the top with company name "ABC Traders Pvt Ltd", address "Kochi, Kerala", and GSTIN "29ABCDE1234F1Z5". Below that a table with clear typed text: Invoice No INV-2025-001, Date 20-Feb-2025, Buyer: Ramesh Textiles, GSTIN 29ABCDE1234F1Z5. Item description: Cotton fabric 100 metres. Taxable value ₹10,000.00, CGST 9% ₹900, SGST 9% ₹900, Total ₹11,800.00. All numbers and text are sharp and readable, no handwriting. Professional document style, good lighting, high contrast black text on white background. No shadows or folds obscuring text.
```

---

## Shorter alternative

```
Indian GST invoice document, white paper, sharp text: Invoice INV-2025-001, ABC Traders, GSTIN 29ABCDE1234F1Z5, taxable ₹10,000, GST 18% ₹1,800, total ₹11,800. Clean, readable, document photo.
```

---

## Tips

- Prefer **text-heavy, document-style** images so the OCR model can read invoice number, date, supplier name, GSTIN, and amounts.
- If the generator produces handwriting or unclear text, add: *"All text must be printed/typed, no handwriting, high resolution."*
- You can also **take a photo** of a real invoice (with sensitive data blurred) or **screenshot** a sample invoice from the web and use that for testing.
